01 / ABOUT US

CURIOSITY → CAPABILITY.

Hands-on engineering culture designed to bridge textbooks to working hardware.

What is RCS?

RCS Robotics Club @ SRC SASTRA brings together students passionate about autonomous robotics, embedded microcontrollers, industrial automation, computer vision and machine intelligence. No prerequisites needed. You learn by designing, wiring, coding and breaking things safely in the lab.
